Our Community Standards

Last updated: April 2026  •  Better Space Platform

Better Space is a safe platform connecting students with verified mental health professionals. To protect everyone, all users, students and professionals alike, must follow these guidelines. Violation of these standards may result in account suspension or removal.

1. Respect & Dignity

  • Treat every person on the platform with respect, regardless of background, belief, or circumstance.
  • Harassment, bullying, discriminatory language, or personal attacks of any kind are strictly prohibited.
  • Use professional and considerate language in all messages and interactions.

2. Honesty & Authenticity

  • Provide accurate information during registration. Impersonation of any person or professional is not allowed.
  • Professionals must only submit genuine, valid credentials for verification.
  • Students must not misrepresent their identity or situation to obtain services.

3. Confidentiality

  • All session content and private messages are confidential. Do not share another user's personal disclosures outside the platform.
  • Professionals are bound by standard clinical confidentiality obligations.
  • Screenshots or recordings of conversations are strictly prohibited.

4. Appropriate Use

  • This platform is for mental health support only. Do not use it for commercial solicitation, spam, or unrelated purposes.
  • In the event of a mental health emergency or immediate risk to life, contact emergency services as this platform is not a crisis hotline.
  • Do not book sessions you do not intend to attend. Cancel in a reasonable timeframe.

5. Privacy

  • Do not share another user's personal information (name, email, contact details) with third parties.
  • Your account credentials are your responsibility, do not share your password with anyone.

6. Feedback & Reviews

  • Reviews and feedback must be honest and based on your genuine experience.
  • Do not post fake, malicious, or retaliatory reviews.

If you witness a violation of these guidelines, please contact the Better Space admin team via the in-platform messaging system. We are committed to keeping this a safe and supportive space for everyone.
